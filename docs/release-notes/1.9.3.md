# BitChord-X 1.9.3

## Connect and Listen Together

- A party no longer needs a signed-in account, and you can start one in a tap, with no QR modal.
- **Connect**: your own devices appear to each other. Hand playback over, use one as the speaker and another as the remote, hide a device or make it refuse control. The mini player says where the sound is coming from while another device is the speaker.
- Connect and parties work together, and a party shows whose it is on your other devices.
- People are grouped by person in the party room, queue covers show who added each song, and queues can be longer.
- Redesigned start/join flow, honest empty states, and notices in your own language (18 locales).
- Joining a Spotify Jam through the bridge, with a QR scanner and invite links that open only the join dialog.
- The party server defaults to the project's own server, and a slow phone no longer holds a whole party back.

## Desktop

- **Explore** now shows regional charts, new releases and daily topics on a genre grid.
- Windows opens in about 3 s, thanks to class-data-sharing archives, and runs on the quick JIT compiler only.
- Connect lives in the player bar as a popover; the More drawer is replaced by Audio output and Connect rows.
- Every floating panel shares the mini player's glass material.
- Single instance, a sensible minimum window size, global keyboard shortcuts and remembered window geometry.
- The PC joins your devices, both as a target and as a remote.

## Player and interface

- The cover dissolves quickly when the track changes and the backdrop fades at the same speed; no dark placeholder while a first-time cover loads.
- The app and its floating bars can take the colours of what is playing. It is off by default.
- Starting new music keeps what you already played behind it in the queue, and a song tapped on a shelf queues the rest of the shelf.
- Redesigned Explore tab with a Bento grid, plus adaptive landscape navigation rails.
- Autoplay and radio can leave out music videos.
- The sleep timer fades the audio out before pausing.
- Smoother start-up, artwork prefetching and fewer redundant skeleton animations.

## Matching

- Artist names tolerate a small spelling slip when a track is matched across sources, and a guest credit no longer earns the wide video duration window.
