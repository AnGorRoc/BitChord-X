# BitChord-X 1.9.3

This release adds Connect, a redesigned Listen Together, Explore charts and a much faster Windows start-up,
and it completes the translations of the desktop app.

## Highlights

- **Connect.** Your own devices see each other and can hand playback over.
- **Listen Together without an account.** Start a party in one tap.
- **Explore with charts.** Regional charts, new releases and daily topics.
- **Windows opens in about 3 seconds.**
- **Complete translations.** The desktop app, Explore and Connect now speak every supported language.

## Connect and Listen Together

- A party no longer needs a signed-in account, and it starts in one tap, with no QR dialog.
- Connect lets your devices hand playback over to each other. Use one device as the speaker and another as
  the remote, hide a device, or make it refuse control.
- While another device is the speaker, the mini player says where the sound is coming from.
- Connect and parties work together, and a party shows whose it is on your other devices.
- People are grouped by person in the party room, queue covers show who added each song, and queues can be
  longer.
- The start and join flow was redesigned, with honest empty states and notices in your own language.
- You can join a Spotify Jam through the bridge, with a QR scanner and invite links that open only the join
  dialog.
- The party server defaults to the project's own server, and a slow phone no longer holds a whole party back.

## Desktop

- **Explore** shows regional charts, new releases and daily topics on a genre grid.
- **Start-up.** Windows opens in about 3 seconds thanks to class-data-sharing archives, and the packaged app
  runs on the quick JIT compiler only.
- **Connect** lives in the player bar as a popover. The More drawer is replaced by Audio output and Connect
  rows.
- Every floating panel shares the mini player's glass material.
- Only one window runs at a time, the window has a sensible minimum size and remembers its position, and
  global keyboard shortcuts were added.
- The PC joins your devices, both as a target and as a remote.

## Player and interface

- The cover dissolves quickly when the track changes and the backdrop fades at the same speed. No dark
  placeholder shows while a first-time cover loads.
- The app and its floating bars can take the colours of what is playing. Grey covers keep a visible tint,
  and settings cards, sheets, alerts and popups follow it. This is off by default.
- The Spotify import dialogs are readable in light mode.
- Starting new music keeps what you already played behind it in the queue, and a song tapped on a shelf
  queues the rest of the shelf.
- The Explore tab was redesigned with a Bento grid, and adaptive landscape navigation rails were added.
- Autoplay and radio can leave out music videos.
- The sleep timer fades the audio out before pausing.
- Start-up is smoother, with artwork prefetching and fewer redundant skeleton animations. Home opens at the top
  and stays there until you scroll it.
- The player opens as one sheet from the mini player too, and the tint of what is playing no longer redraws the
  whole app while it fades.
- The BitChord mark at the top of Home is made of the same glass or frost as the floating controls.
- Autoplay keeps a stock of 15 suggestions, 10 of them queued, and continues from the last track you chose.
  The PC does the same.
- Artist pages are cached and fetched ahead of a tap from search results. An album page shows the artist's other
  releases and similar artists, on the phone and on the PC.
- The app notices when the connection is back after a spell offline instead of staying in offline mode.
- With no connection, only what is saved on the phone can be played. Songs that are not downloaded or cached
  whole are greyed out and cannot be played or queued, playlists show the playable songs first and the rest
  below, and a new **Offline music** playlist at the top of the Library lists everything the phone can play,
  downloads first and then cached songs. Offline autoplay keeps playing from saved songs, preferring the same
  artist, album or download. A small cloud-off icon in the top bar shows the state (tap it to leave manual offline
  mode). All of it goes back to normal by itself when the connection returns, and each playlist returns to its own
  order.
- Pages you have read once (artists, albums, playlists, Spotify) are kept on disk and open at once, even with no
  connection, and the library is read ahead. The PC does the same.
- Home opens whole on its first frame and stays still while it refreshes. Background playback no longer drops
  the app's presence, and autoplay retries when playback starts with a short tail.

## Matching

- Artist names tolerate a small spelling slip when a track is matched across sources.
- A guest credit no longer earns the wide video duration window.

## Translations

- The desktop-only strings, the Explore and charts screens and the Connect notices are translated into all
  supported languages.
- Texts that used to fall back to English in the app, in the desktop UI and in the shared UI are now
  translated.

## Changes to be aware of

- **Closing the desktop window now quits the app.** Previously it stayed in the tray by default. To keep the
  old behaviour, turn on **Keep running in background when closed** in Settings. Turning it on also turns the
  tray icon on.
- **Tinting with what is playing is off by default.** Turn it on in Settings if you want it.
- **Anonymous telemetry is on by default, in three levels.** The first time the app opens it explains what is sent
  and where to change it. **Full** sends a stable install ID, the version, the platform, when the app is open and,
  if something fails, the error details with the device model and OS version, with home folders, user names and URL
  queries removed. **Minimal** sends only that the app is open, under an ID that changes every day, and counts errors
  by type with no details. **Off** sends nothing. It never sends your account, IP address, listening history or
  playlists. Change it with **Anonymous telemetry and error reports** in Settings, on the phone and on the PC. How
  the server treats each level is in `docs/telemetry.md`.
